About the Event

The Boca Lax Showdown is an exciting weekend youth lacrosse tournament held at Patch Reef Park in Boca Raton, Florida! Open to boys and girls teams in U12 to U18 divisions, it features competitive bracket play, skills challenges, MVP awards, and a fun post-tournament celebration with food trucks, music, and raffles.

Whether you're a player, coach, parent, or fan, come join the action. It's family-friendly, supports growing lacrosse in South Florida, and raises funds for local youth programs. No experience needed to spectate — just bring your team spirit!

What to Expect

Get Ready for Non-Stop Lacrosse Action!

  • Competitive Games: Bracket-style play with top local teams battling it out
  • Skills Contests: Fastest shot, accuracy challenge, and more for prizes
  • Family Fun: Food trucks, music, spectator seating, and post-event party

Event Schedule

Bracket Play & Championship Games

Teams compete in divisions throughout the day, leading to exciting finals on Sunday afternoon. Watch intense face-offs, dodges, and goals!

Awards & MVP Recognition

Top performers get MVP awards, plus team trophies. Celebrate with photos and cheers after the games.

Skills Challenges

Test your stick skills in fun contests like fastest shot and accuracy — open to all participants and spectators to watch.
